When something goes wrong

Could not save "Trip packing list": EACCES permission denied

What it means

Your computer refused to let Driftwood write to the library folder — usually because the folder was moved into a protected location.

How to fix it

Choose SettingsLibraryChange… and pick a folder inside your Documents.
This note was changed outside Driftwood. Reload?

What it means

Another app (or a sync tool) edited the file while it was open here.

How to fix it

Click Reload to take the newer version. Your in-app edits are kept in EditRestore Previous.

Spot the mistake: Sam wants their notes on a new laptop

1Install Driftwood on the new laptop
2Delete the library folder from the old laptop
3Copy the library folder to a USB stick
4On the new laptop: Settings › Library › Change… and pick the copied folder
